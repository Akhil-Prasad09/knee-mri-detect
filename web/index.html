Knee MRI abnormality detection

Runs the three per-plane EfficientNet-B3 models from knee-mri-detect in your browser, then averages them like the app does. Validation AUC for the three-plane ensemble: abnormal 0.943, ACL tear 0.960, meniscus tear 0.847.

Research demo, not a medical device. Do not use it for diagnosis. Your files never leave this device: everything runs locally in the page. No example scans are included, because the MRNet dataset's license does not allow sharing them.
